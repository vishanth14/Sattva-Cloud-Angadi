# PROJECT CONTEXT: Sattva Cloud Angadi

## Project Name & Purpose
- **Name:** Sattva Cloud Angadi
- **Purpose:** Premium digital heritage-commerce experience for traditional Indian products made from copper, brass, bronze, and other heritage materials.

## Core Product Principle
We do not treat heritage as decoration. Every product must communicate its material, cultural context, traditional ritual, craftsmanship, maker, usage, care, and long-term significance.

### Experience Qualities
- Indian-rooted, not stereotypically "ethnic"
- Premium, minimal, contemporary, and culturally meaningful
- Story-driven rather than transaction-driven
- Educational without feeling like a museum
- Modern technology supporting heritage, never overpowering it

## Primary User Journey
Discover → Understand → Connect → Purchase → Care → Continue the Story

## Core Business Rules
1. Product discovery must go beyond categories and support discovery through materials and rituals.
2. Product pages must prioritize meaning and heritage alongside commerce.
3. Every product is a cultural object, not merely an SKU.
4. Material, ritual, craftsmanship, artisan identity, and care are core product information.
5. Heritage must be integrated into UX, not added as decorative content.
6. Modern technical features must preserve and transmit traditional knowledge.
7. Avoid generic e-commerce patterns wherever a more meaningful ROOTS-specific experience is possible.
8. Do not overclaim wellness or health benefits; present traditional practices and cultural context responsibly.

## Key ROOTS Features
- **Material Discovery:** Discover products by Material (Copper, Brass, Bronze, etc.).
- **Ritual Discovery:** Discover products by Ritual (Morning, Kitchen, Dining, Celebration, Everyday Living).
- **Flagship Integration:** Every flagship product combines Material + Ritual + Maker + Craft + Care.
- **Heritage Passport:** Persistent identity containing origin, material, craft, maker, and ownership context.
- **ROOTS Companion:** Contextual heritage and product-care assistant.
- **Care Guide:** Frames ageing and patina as part of the object's lifecycle where culturally/materially appropriate.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Separate Heritage Story page/module
- QR Product Identity
- Packaging Design
- Heritage Card
- QR Continuity
- Authentication
- Real payment gateway
- Admin panel
- Complex backend infrastructure
- Database unless absolutely required

## Technical Principle
This is a hackathon MVP. Prefer static structured data and clean service abstractions over unnecessary backend complexity. Build for a polished, functional demo rather than production scale.
