## ✨ About

**Sattva Cloud Angadi** is a digital marketplace and heritage experience designed to reconnect users with India's traditional **materials, crafts, artisans, products, and everyday practices**.

Instead of presenting traditional products as isolated objects, Sattva connects each product with the story behind it:

> **Material → Making → Artisan → Practice → Knowledge → Product**

The platform brings **commerce and cultural understanding** together in one digital experience.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional Indian products often reach modern marketplaces without the context that gives them meaning.

Users may see a copper vessel, brass lamp, Kansa utensil, or terracotta product without discovering:

* Where the material comes from
* How the product is made
* Who makes it
* How it has traditionally been used
* Why the material or craft matters
* How the product should be cared for

This creates a gap between **purchasing a traditional object and understanding its heritage**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Sattva Cloud Angadi transforms the conventional:

**BROWSE → BUY**

experience into:

**DISCOVER → LEARN → EXPERIENCE → CONNECT → PURCHASE → CARE**

Users can explore traditional materials, discover associated crafts and artisans, understand traditional practices, and explore products connected to that heritage.

# 🌾 Key Features

### 1. 🧱 Material-First Discovery

Explore traditional Indian materials before browsing individual products.

Featured materials include:

* Copper
* Brass
* Bronze
* Kansa / Bell Metal
* Iron
* Terracotta

---

### 2. 🔨 From Object to Origin

Products are connected to their origins through:

**Material → Craft → Artisan → Tradition → Everyday Use**

This helps users understand not just **what** they are buying, but **where it came from and how it was made**.

---

### 3. ⚒️ The Making of Kansa

Explore the traditional making process of **Kansa / Bell Metal** objects through key stages:

**Alloying → Casting → Forging → Turning → Finishing**

The experience highlights the role of material knowledge, craftsmanship, time, and technique.

---

### 4. 👨‍🎨 Artisan Stories

Artisans are presented as an important part of the product experience.

Featured craft communities include:

| Artisan Community            | Location               | Craft                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Kansa Craftsmen of Kantapada | Odisha                 | Kansa / Bell Metal Forging  |
| Sthapathi Bronze Craftsmen   | Swamimalai, Tamil Nadu | Lost-Wax Bronze Casting     |
| Potter Community of Molela   | Rajasthan              | Terracotta Vessel Making    |
| Brass Artisans of Moradabad  | Uttar Pradesh          | Brass Casting & Finishing   |
| Temple Craft Artisans        | Thrissur, Kerala       | Copper & Brass Temple Craft |

---

### 5. 🪔 Practices & Rituals

Connect traditional objects with the practices in which they have historically been used.

Examples include:

* 💧 Morning Copper Water
* 🪔 The Evening Diya
* 🍽️ Kansa Dining

---

### 6. 🤖 ROOTS — Knowledge Companion

**ROOTS** is a conversational knowledge companion integrated into the Sattva experience.

Users can explore questions about:

* Traditional materials
* Craft techniques
* Product care
* Cultural practices
* Craft origins
* Historical context
* Traditional uses

Where applicable, information is presented through separate perspectives:

**📜 Traditional Knowledge**
Traditional uses, practices, and cultural context.

**🌿 Ayurvedic Context**
Relevant traditional Ayurvedic perspectives.

**🔬 Scientific Context**
Modern scientific understanding.

This distinction helps separate traditional knowledge from modern scientific evidence.
